Commands

Type what the arms should do, for example raise left hand, bend elbows, move hands forward 5 cm, twist right wrist clockwise, open grippers, wave, go home. Join steps with then. The arms move at 30 degrees per second; STOP works as usual.

HEBI Mobile I/O

Hold Move (B1) to move. A3 sets the gripper while moving, B2 opens, B3 closes, B8 homes. Calibrate once by moving the phone about 10 cm up, forward and left while holding Move.
